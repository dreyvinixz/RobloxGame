## Implemented
- Health and state are server-created model attributes (`VehicleHealth`, `VehicleMaxHealth`, `VehicleState`). `DamageService` calls `VehicleService.ApplyHit` only after CombatRequestService accepts a shot and the server raycast hits the vehicle.
- Server collision raycasts apply health damage at speed. Destruction clears driver controls, ejects the driver, disables the seat, and stops server movement. Entry and input stepping require `Active`.
- A nearby `VehicleAction("Reset")` after destruction replaces the old model at its saved spawn pivot. Reset has a global cooldown; accepted shooter damage is rate-limited per attacker. The client sends no health, damage, or transform.
- The vehicle remains procedural and uses no external assets or paid services.
