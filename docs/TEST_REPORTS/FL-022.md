# FL-022 — Gameplay HUD (partial)

**Date:** 2026-10-15

## Implemented

- Health text/bar follows the local Humanoid and changes color at low health.
- Existing weapon ammo/reload display now scales with viewport size and includes selected weapon name.
- Team scores, round number, phase, and countdown read server-owned `MatchState` attributes.
- Round intermission shows winner/tie and final score.
- Hit marker is shown only after a server-confirmed humanoid hit through `HitConfirmed`.
- Labels use viewport-relative layout and text size constraints.

## Studio device captures — 2026-10-15

The Studio Device Simulator was run in landscape at two phone resolutions. Both captures show the waiting-for-players state with 100 HP, the 0–0 team score, round status, and rifle ammunition `30 / 30`.

| Device | Device resolution | Studio viewport | Capture |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| iPhone 17 Pro | 874×402 | 750×381 | `FL-022-iphone17pro-landscape.jpg` |
| iPhone 7 | 667×375 | 666×374 | `FL-022-iphone7-landscape.jpg` |

The first small-screen capture exposed a wrapped/truncated `AGUARDANDO JOGADORES` status and an ammo box overlapping the Roblox touch jump button. The round status was widened and kept to one line; weapon status/ammo were moved above the touch controls and given a 14 px minimum text constraint. Final screenshots show the complete round status and ammo label. Runtime geometry at 666×374 reports no ammo/jump or status/ammo overlap.

The first Studio Play attempt also found that `WeaponController` was waiting for `FirstPersonViewmodel`, which was absent from the Studio DataModel even though it exists in the Rojo source tree. I synchronized that tracked module exactly from the FL-022 branch; the controller and ammo HUD then initialized. The play console showed the expected controller/HUD startup messages with no script error.

These captures cover the waiting state only. An active round countdown and the end-of-round result panel were not visually captured, so those states remain a review item.
